## 3. Reopening trigger

The Programme Disposition Closure Review Section 16 expressly makes its reopening examples illustrative, non-exhaustive, and non-authorizing, and includes “another specific, precisely-stated development.” Section 17 prohibits desire for a winner, passage of time, an unchanged rerun, standards-lowering, least-bad selection, untested combinations, absence of new evidence alone, and pressure from the paused programme's own Unit 4.

The Knowledge Discoverability dependency is materially different from every prohibited ground: it is a separately governed downstream programme with an accepted, non-waivable live-verification requirement; two preserved attempts could not exercise promotion-to-recall; and the accepted factual record traces the observed Attempt 2 action-selection failure to the existing PF01/R01-direct problem surface. This is a concrete operational consequence, not new remedy-comparison evidence and not a demand for a winner.

## 6. Alternative-model and qualification boundary

Unit 3-A Section 11 states that one isolated execution does not qualify a model and that qualification attaches to the immutable model/provider/configuration under the full governed process. The prior DQ4 comparison is exploratory and doubly confounded; no alternative-model experiment occurred in Unit 3-C through Unit 3-E.

The Assessment accurately states that no model, including the current `llama3.2:3b` configuration, has completed this programme's qualification-tier process. It classifies an informal substitution now as an unauthorized bypass and permits Family F diagnostic work only after fresh, expressly scoped governance. It neither recommends nor authorizes `qwen2.5-coder:7b` or any other model.

## 7. Proportionality

The Family A Extension Review found the governance overhead of a new live campaign high and roughly fixed and the probability of changing Unit 3-E's disposition plausible but not favored. The new Knowledge Discoverability dependency does not change those evidentiary facts.

The Assessment correctly identifies the limited change: continued inaction now has a concrete downstream cost. That change supports making a fresh status decision; it does not manufacture remedy evidence, improve a candidate, lower a threshold, or make a campaign automatically proportionate.

## 8. Recommended Reopening Decision

A standalone programme-level Reopening Decision is a proportionate next act because the programme is paused rather than closed and the Closure Review requires fresh, specifically justified governance before reopening. The proposed act is limited to deciding PAUSED versus ACTIVE. If ACTIVE, it may name one already-classified path as eligible to proceed only to its own Planning Review.

The Assessment's entry and stop conditions prevent that decision from becoming a disguised Scope Lock, Implementation Plan, remedy selection, campaign approval, model run, or evidence-pooling exercise. “REMAIN PAUSED, TRIGGER NOTED” remains an expressly lawful outcome. No source requires an ACTIVE outcome merely because the trigger is satisfied.
